# Meio Alts

## 1.1.0

- Rested XP counts Forever's Well Rested Legacy talent: faster filling and a higher cap, so
  "full in" is right for characters that have it (the tooltip names the talent and the cap)
- At login, one chat line names your alts that are fully rested or will be within a day,
  each once until you play it again (`/alts rest off` to turn it off)
- Characters on your other WoW account: `/alts add Name` puts them in the mailbox To list
  (under Other account) and in the list. Forever won't let you friend them, so nothing else
  fills their name in
- Right-click a column heading to hide it (or `/alts hide Mail`): the window narrows to fit
- The gold total counts what you can actually mail: this realm and faction, with mail. Hover
  it for each realm and faction and the grand total. Characters elsewhere sort last, faded
  (`/alts elsewhere off` hides them)
- Hover shows unspent Legacy points

## 1.0.1

- Same-name characters on different realms no longer overwrite each other: the realm is
  blank at login on Forever, so characters are now told apart by their server too. Existing
  records are kept and only merged when name, class and race match
- Expired mail drops out of the mailbox gold and its warning clears
- At max level, gold, zone and the rest are recorded again (only XP is skipped)

## 1.0.0

- First release: every character in one list beside the Esc menu and in `/alts`, with
  level, XP and rested XP (still filling while logged out), gold, mail, where they are and
  when you last played them; hover a character for bags, durability, professions and more
- Mailbox: pick one of your characters to send to, and Alt-click a bag item to attach it
